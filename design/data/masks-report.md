# Terrain masks: build and validation report (Task 4)

Generated by `python3 -I design/tools/build_masks.py reference/vassal-extracted/images design/data src/data/masks --report design/data/masks-report.md --audit design/data/masks-audit.json`. Masks: `src/data/masks/<id>.json` (res 12 cells per hex radius, x0 = y0 = -1, 276 x 228 cells, RLE `cls` = MASK class codes of `src/engine/types.ts`, `level` = hill level 0-4). They classify the drawn terrain; no image data is stored.

## Method

- Overlays are masked out before classifying: grid lines (2.3 px band along every hexside), hex labels, centre dots, objective circles (detected by their red), the art of transcribed hexside features (fence 9 px, hedge 12 px, wall 16 px, cliff 10 px either side of the hexside) and the map-edge border.
- Pixels are classified into elements (open ground of level 0-3, foliage, brush ground of level 0-2, field, roof, water, marsh, gully, road, railway, bridge) by a naive-Bayes colour model learnt from the transcriptions (pixels round the centre dot of every certain hex; road and railway hexside crossings; bridges), smoothed over 5 x 5 px. Hill levels absent from a map's transcription are not offered (brown roof halves look like level-2/3 tints); dark tints enclosed by roof outline are roof; thin strips of roof colour (hill-edge and cliff shading) are dropped.
- Cells: woods = foliage areas >= 1000 px; tree-sized blobs (300-1000 px) standing on plain ground are orchard trees and the orchard is the closed region round 4+ of them; brush = broad areas of brush ground, or broad dense fields of small bushes (pale brush, vineyards); water bodies survive a radius-3-cell opening, narrower water is stream; bridges are deck cells with water on two opposite sides; roads/gullies/buildings must be at least 2 cells thick, railways and gullies long; small patches of brush/orchard/marsh merge into their surroundings. Road and railway corridors are widened so that every road/railway cell lies in a full 2 x 2 block (LOS along them stays inside).
- Trails are not a class: the dashes are ignored and the cleared corridor drawn round a trail in woods stays open ground, as drawn.
- Hill level per cell from the ground tint (3 x 3 cell window; 7 x 7 inside brush, whose vineyard stripes alternate shades), level regions under 40 cells merged into their surroundings. Under woods, roofs, fields and roads the level is that of the nearest visible ground of the same hex, else the nearest anywhere, and never above the hex's centre level (FAQ: a crest hex has its centre dot on the higher level, so a higher terrace inside a hex must be visible).
- Map 5w is the winter art of Map 5 (same geography: 97.5% of 5w's blue pixels fall in Map 5's water/stream/marsh cells at zero offset). Its mask is Map 5's; it is validated against map-5w.json.

Validation per hex (cells whose centre lies in the hex): classes present (ignoring specks under 2% of the hex), the highest-ranked type (T78.1 hierarchy; road/railway count as Open, bridge as Bridge) vs the transcription; the level of the centre cell vs the transcribed elevation; crest = two levels each over 5% of the hex; multi-hex buildings = Building hexes whose roof art is continuous across the shared hexside (roof pixels 4-6 px either side of the grid line along 3+ consecutive samples of 43). A hex whose centre level is above a neighbour's is a crest hex as well when its contour runs on the shared hexside (the cliff case); every such hex either holds a sliver of the lower level or has the level change exactly on the hexside.
